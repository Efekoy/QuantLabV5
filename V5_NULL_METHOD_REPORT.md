# V5 null and multiple-testing method

Status: **precalibration implementation, no final freeze**. Fixed-family daily-stream bootstrap/stepdown and sequential stopping are implemented and tested in `quantlab5/v5/inference.py`; the executable 30-family A/B/C search and market-level replay are connected on synthetic markets. The controlled DISCOVERY nuisance worker, symmetric zero-edge generator, executable calibration grid and family-direction validation rule are specified in `V5_PRECALIBRATION_SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`. The fixed-stream and four-neighbor analyses below are historical planning diagnostics; the precalibration protocol governs operational differences. No new real DISCOVERY strategy outcome or sealed partition has been examined, and no calibrated adaptive full-search p-value exists yet.

## Estimand and statistic

The primary unit is a **session**, not a trade. Save each candidate's daily net P&L in R (zero on inactive days), daily trade count, risk-budget execution mask, and common session index. The main statistic is the studentized mean of daily net R with a 20-session heteroskedasticity/autocorrelation-consistent (HAC) long-run variance estimate. Also report net R per executed trade, median daily R, active sessions, annual contributions, baseline/stress cost, and a drift/time-of-day-matched excess statistic. A tiny denominator is floored by a predeclared variance floor; a degenerate all-zero stream is ineligible. Preserve synchronized candidate streams when resampling so overlap and correlation enter multiplicity correction.

## Fixed Stage A universe: stream bootstrap

Stage A has a fixed, fully enumerated 30-mechanism universe before any result. Recenter each candidate's daily stream to mean zero for the least-favorable boundary null `mean ≤ 0`; do not bootstrap raw positive means as a null. Apply a **common** stationary-bootstrap session index to every candidate, expected block length 20 sessions. Resample 10 and 40 as sensitivity checks; main decisions use 20. Recompute HAC t for every candidate and the maximum across the entire Stage A library, including weak and losing candidates. This is a White Reality Check style max-t test for the fixed library. A Hansen-SPA-style poor-model exclusion can be reported only as a sensitivity analysis with its exact recentering rule frozen and calibrated on zero-edge worlds; it cannot silently replace the conservative main test. Report `(1+exceedances)/(1+B)` Monte Carlo p and the maximum-statistic reference distribution. For candidate-level claims use Romano-Wolf-style stepdown max-t across the fixed family: at each step resample the joint vector of hypotheses still under test, compare their maximum, and enforce monotone adjusted p-values. A candidate must have adjusted p ≤0.05 for a Stage A inferential claim. The bootstrap's weak stationarity and adequate-block assumptions must be checked; this is approximate inference, not exact exchangeability.

Stage A family expansion may use a family maximum adjusted across all 30 mechanisms at p ≤0.10 plus ≥120 trades, ≥6 active calendar years, positive stress-cost net, and a positive drift/time-matched excess estimate. The 0.10 gate allocates exploration, **not** a supported-edge claim. All qualifying families expand; no family-count cap.

## Hierarchical fixed-library diagnostic

`quantlab5/v5/hierarchy.py` implements a *conditional* four-level diagnostic on 30 families with four nearby rules each. Its global statistic is the larger of the maximum exact-rule HAC t and the maximum family-mean HAC t. A common-index, centered stationary bootstrap calibrates that statistic. At the family level, the bootstrap maximum across all 30 family means adjusts the target family's p-value. Each family mean is computed from **daily R streams**, so overlapping or duplicated trades do not become independent observations. A supported neighborhood additionally requires at least three of four neighboring rules to have positive full-period means and the family mean to be positive in both chronological halves. These cluster conditions are frozen diagnostics; they do not themselves provide a p-value. The representative is the rule whose centered daily path is closest to the within-family mean path, with lowest ID breaking ties. This selects a deterministic specimen, but confers no individual exact-rule significance.

The fixed-library diagnostic compares a one-rule HAC test, a one-rule block bootstrap, unadjusted block-bootstrap testing of the target in a 120-rule panel, Holm adjustment of the continuous HAC marginal p-values, exact-rule Romano-Wolf stepdown, and the hierarchical global/family/neighborhood procedure. The one-rule bootstrap and unadjusted target-in-panel p-values are algebraically identical because the same target stream and common resample indices are used; the multiplicity penalty begins at Holm or joint max/stepdown. With 99 bootstrap resamples, a marginal bootstrap p cannot be below 0.01 and is **unsuitable for 120-way Holm**; the HAC marginal comparator avoids that finite-grid artifact. It requires HAC calibration, and is a comparison rather than the proposed final adaptive search test.

The hierarchy is currently a diagnostic of a fixed library, not a calibrated claim about the adaptive A→B→C strategy search. The eventual market-level replay must reconstruct every family's features and neighbors, rerun all eligibility/expansion choices inside each world, and apply the **same** global/family/cluster/representative decision rule to that world. No fixed-stream p-value can stand in for this replay.

## Adaptive Stages B and C

Stages B/C are conditional on Stage A outcomes. A bootstrap over only the candidates generated in the real market ignores this adaptive selection and understates search error. Their stream-bootstrap max-t is a **screening diagnostic only**. For a discovery-wide p-value on the complete A→B→C algorithm, replay all stages and every decision in each null world. The same world must generate its own expanded families. If full replay is not completed, record discovery evidence as exploratory and rely on the independent 2019–2022 validation gate for formal candidate claims. No selected-library bootstrap p-value is described as full-search-adjusted.

The full-market null library has two purposes. A zero-edge simulator calibrated to DISCOVERY-only structure measures unconditional false positives and planted-edge power. A strong path-preserving comparator reorders jointly sampled 30-minute NQ/ES blocks within the same session-clock/volatility strata, reconstitutes causal bars, and reruns the full algorithm. It retains local trends and some real edge by design, so its p-value answers a **stronger, different question**: is the observed timing more distinctive than generic short-path continuation? It is not an edge-free type-I reference. Independent NQ/ES sign flips may be a weak diagnostic, never the sole main null. The path comparator should preserve market co-movement, intraday seasonality and cost geometry; diagnostics quantify deviations in volatility, volume, gaps and correlations. A bad surrogate is rejected before interpretation.

## Independent validation and audits

Freeze every discovery candidate, model coefficient, training cutoff, cost model, parameter neighborhood and deterministic representative before validation opens. Apply all frozen candidates unchanged to 2019–2022. The proposed family-first validation computes a daily ensemble stream for each frozen neighborhood and adjusts family tests across **all frozen families**, irrespective of discovery rank or overlap. A family/cluster may be supported while its member exact-rule p-values remain individually nonsignificant; the preselected representative is reported as a representative, not an individually significant rule. Require adjusted family p ≤0.05, the frozen neighborhood stability criterion, positive stress-cost net and positive drift/time-matched excess, plus the representative's ≥40 executed trades and ≥2 positive calendar years. Positivity conditions cannot rescue a nonsignificant family p-value. All qualifying family representatives survive without a numerical cap; if a family is supported but its preselected representative fails, leave it inconclusive rather than choosing another from validation. The actual A/B/C neighborhood topology and size calibration remain unresolved, so this is a **design proposal**, not an operational validation gate. A positive result failing these gates is INCONCLUSIVE; a materially negative result is REJECTED as defined in the preregistration. Later audits test the entire representative cohort individually, with joint adjusted p-values across it. Audit 2 is a replication/falsification window with low power, not a fresh selection opportunity. No audit result changes membership or risk.

## Sequential Monte Carlo, fixed before results

For the one preregistered global statistic, generate batches to cumulative `B=50,100,200,500` using fixed seeds and a fixed ordering. At each look, form an exact two-sided Clopper-Pearson interval for the exceedance probability with **per-look error 0.0025**; the four-look union error is at most 0.01. Stop and declare `p<0.05` only if the interval's upper end is below 0.05; stop and declare `p>0.05` only if its lower end is above 0.05. At 500, if neither condition holds, label Monte Carlo inference **UNRESOLVED**; never infer from the raw fraction alone. Report the plus-one p estimate and interval. For the fixed candidate-level stepdown inference, run a fixed 500 resamples and report Monte Carlo uncertainty; if any adjusted-p decision is within 0.01 of 0.05, increase to 2000 **for all candidates in that family**, with fresh preassigned seeds, or leave it unresolved if compute is unavailable. Do not selectively continue a favored candidate. Calibration worlds use disjoint seeds and replicate the stopping rule itself.

## Limitations requiring audit

Overlapping trades, nonstationarity, heavy-tailed R, and endogenous skip masks may invalidate simple daily resampling. A block length chosen after seeing p-values is forbidden. Resampling real strategy P&L preserves shared regime effects but cannot validate novel feature causality; feature future-rewrite tests and full replay are separate requirements. A strong path-preserving null can retain true intrablock continuation and therefore fail to reject even a real edge. V4's known later outcomes compromise human-level blinding despite the V5 software gate. Label all historical claims accordingly.
