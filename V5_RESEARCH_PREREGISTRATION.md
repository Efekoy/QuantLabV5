# QuantLabV5 research preregistration — review draft

**Status: IMPLEMENTATION HOLD / REVIEW REQUIRED; NO STRATEGY SEARCH AUTHORIZED.** All 30 Stage A signal families and adaptive A/B/C expansion execute on synthetic markets. The independent stream-proxy replication found 77.3% survival for a 0.10R plateau at approximately 500 trades/year (95% CI 72.8–81.3%); this is planning evidence only. The controlled DISCOVERY nuisance-access design and binding executable calibration criteria are in `V5_PRECALIBRATION_SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`. That protocol must be frozen before any new real DISCOVERY read. Full market-level calibration and final size/power gates remain incomplete. No validation or historical-audit partition may be opened for this work.

## 1. Question, information boundary and estimand

Can a new, causally measurable intraday NQ/ES state improve the timing of NQ continuation after costs, survive independent 2019–2022 validation, and remain viable through two historical audits? The main predictive estimand is **mean daily net R** of a frozen rule, with net R per executed trade and a same-time/same-direction drift benchmark reported alongside. Prop feasibility is a separate estimand under integer MNQ sizing and illustrative account profiles. Historical audits are sequential tests, not development data.

Use only DISCOVERY (2010-06-08–2018-12-31) for design calibration and search; VALIDATION (2019-01-02–2022-12-30) only after discovery freeze; HISTORICAL_AUDIT_1 (2023-01-03–2025-12-31) only after final cohort/portfolio freeze; HISTORICAL_AUDIT_2 (2026 through the common NQ/ES endpoint 2026-08-10) only after the audit-1 report freeze. LIVE_FORWARD begins only after final V5 freeze and uses data strictly later than its timestamp. Stage access is enforced by `config/stage_policy.yaml`; every read uses `load_view` and the ledger. Prior V4 results are methodological context, never tuning data for V5.

## 2. Immutable execution and costs

Signal at completed bar `t`, earliest entry at the next bar open. Retain the bootstrap engine's conservative stop/target same-bar resolution, tick rounding, contract roll exclusion, and flat-by-session rule. Use registered NQ/ES synchronized 1-minute OHLCV only. A signal with unavailable feature history is skipped and counted. Baseline/stress cost scenarios are in `config/costs.yaml`; report gross/moderate/baseline/stress, with baseline governing inferential eligibility and stress governing robustness. Cost and MNQ commission uncertainty must be reported; the current $1 MNQ round-trip commission is an unvalidated placeholder and cannot support a real-firm claim.

## 3. Candidate universe and hierarchical search

`V5_STRATEGY_CATALOG.md` defines 30 Stage A mechanism prototypes. A fixed simple continuation anchor is a control, not a new V5 discovery. Each prototype and its long/short variants is evaluated; every tested ID and result is retained. Stage A's joint max-t bootstrap uses the complete fixed universe, with family-wise p ≤0.10, ≥120 discovery trades, ≥6 active calendar years, positive stress net and positive drift/time excess for **family expansion**. All families satisfying that conjunction enter Stage B; none are removed by a numerical cap. Mechanisms failing the gate remain reported, including underpowered ones.

Stage B makes only one-factor-at-a-time changes: causal state lookback half/double where a lookback is defined, and state threshold 0.75×/1.25× the prototype where a magnitude threshold is defined; fixed categorical mechanisms omit inapplicable axes. Use the same common stop and exit. Stage C tests meaningful interactions only: each Stage B family winner by **predeclared** within-family daily t, with ties by canonical candidate ID, is crossed with (a) a previous-session RTH-open return continuation anchor and (b) a path-occupation continuation anchor. A C15 anchor may be added only to a family whose Stage B event does **not** already require C15; a generated intersection identical to its parent signal is omitted as a duplicate. Every interaction must produce a distinct frozen specification and signal rule. The Stage B winner choice and Stage C conditionality are replayed in full surrogate worlds. These bounded changes prevent an old stop/indicator grid disguised as novelty. No extra lookbacks, thresholds, exits, or pairwise combinations may be added after seeing results. A family can have no Stage B winner; then it generates no Stage C member. Stage C winner selection does **not** discard other qualifying Stage B candidates from validation.

Before the first real search, machine-readable config must enumerate all applicable axes, tie handling, feature fitting windows, model seeds, conditional branches, exact ID fingerprints and maximum possible candidate count. This text is not sufficient to run Stage C until those are pinned. All candidate streams are retained for null inference; low-scoring candidates cannot disappear from the denominator. Behavorial clusters annotate `duplicate_of` but all scientific qualifiers advance. No top-N cap anywhere.

## 4. Power and null gates

The assumptions-only table in `V5_POWER_CALIBRATION_REPORT.md` demonstrates that 0.01–0.03R individual edges are usually underpowered. The binding executable market calibration uses all-null worlds and 0.05R, 0.075R, 0.10R and 0.15R plants at 100, 250 and 500 opportunities per year across four shapes, as specified in `V5_PRECALIBRATION_SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`. Earlier 0.01–0.03R stream panels remain diagnostic. Do not lower alpha, size or power criteria after the precalibration science freeze. Global discovery p for adaptive search is claimable only after complete search replay; otherwise discovery is exploratory. If executable calibration fails after nuisance access, stop rather than redesign using DISCOVERY information.

The executable validation rule is specified in `V5_PRECALIBRATION_SCIENCE_PROTOCOL.md`: family-direction daily ensembles are adjusted across every frozen group, while every member satisfying individual economic and execution gates survives. Representative nomination is an annotation and never excludes another qualifier. Family support does not confer individual exact-rule significance. The complete adaptive A/B/C search is replayed on each market-level null world.

## 5. Discovery selection and freeze

An individual candidate is `DISCOVERY_QUALIFYING` if it has ≥120 executed baseline trades, trades in ≥6 distinct calendar years, baseline net daily R >0, stress-cost net >0, and positive matched drift/time excess. For Stage A an adjusted discovery p ≤0.05 adds an inferential label; for adaptive B/C the full-search surrogate p ≤0.05 is required for a discovery-wide inferential label. However **all** qualifying candidates, inferentially labelled or not, advance to validation. This broad advancement deliberately lets validation test uncertain discovery candidates, and the validation max-t adjusts over the entire frozen list. The freeze contains `qualifying`, `candidates`, complete ID/rule hashes, duplicate annotations, the full tested universe fingerprint, rules and selection trace. Set `candidates = qualifying`; no top cap. If no candidates qualify, freeze the empty lists and report a negative search. Do not create a best-effort cohort.

## 6. Validation and scientific cohort

Run every frozen discovery qualifier unchanged in 2019–2022. Before opening validation, freeze each candidate's mechanism family, direction, parameters and discovery nominee annotation. For each family-direction group, average its members' common-calendar daily R streams and apply common-index stationary-bootstrap Romano-Wolf stepdown across all frozen groups with 500 resamples, block length 20, HAC lag 20 and alpha 0.05. If any adjusted p is within 0.01 of 0.05, rerun all groups with 2,000 fresh preassigned resamples; a group still within 0.01 is unresolved. Require adjusted group p ≤0.05, positive ensemble mean in each chronological half, and positive full-period means for at least `ceil(0.75 × group size)` members. Every member of a supported group survives if it has ≥40 executed trades, ≥2 active years, positive baseline mean daily R, positive stress net and positive matched drift/time excess. Nominee status never removes another qualifying member. Family support is not individual exact-rule significance. Unresolved Monte Carlo decisions do not qualify. A positive result failing these gates is `INCONCLUSIVE/UNDERPOWERED`; a rule with upper one-sided 95% HAC confidence bound <0 is `REJECTED`; otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE`. Validation survival is a selection result, not proof of transport to 2023 onward.

Every survivor becomes a member of the **scientific cohort**. Evaluate each individually in both historical audits, irrespective of what happened to it in audit 1. For each audit report baseline/stress net, daily HAC estimate and interval, adjusted p across the entire survivor set, trade count, annual/monthly contributions, matched drift/time excess and four-budget MNQ coverage. `SUPPORTED` in an audit requires adjusted one-sided p ≤0.05, positive stress net, positive matched excess, and ≥20 trades; `REJECTED` requires upper one-sided 95% HAC confidence bound <0 or a documented execution failure making the frozen rule non-executable; otherwise `INCONCLUSIVE/UNDERPOWERED`. These labels apply **separately to each audit**. Audit 2's short length will often yield INCONCLUSIVE. No strategy is removed from audit 2 because audit 1 disappointed. An overall replication claim requires support in both audits; otherwise state the two labels, without inventing an overall winner.

The audit-1 report, including all individual results and classifications, is frozen before audit 2 opens. Audit 2 cannot influence any candidate, cost, risk, or portfolio parameter. The true live-forward cohort is the frozen scientific cohort; no retroactive audit selection.

## 7. MNQ risk budgets and prop portfolios

From DISCOVERY onward report each candidate at $250/$300/$350/$400 maximum planned stop risk. Sizing is deterministic integer MNQ, using the existing `SKIP` policy when even one micro exceeds budget; include modeled entry/exit friction in the recorded realized risk and report any slippage beyond planned risk. For each budget record total signal opportunities, valid stop signals, one-MNQ floor skips, policy/cap skips, executed signals, `executed/valid` coverage, contract distribution, baseline/stress net, drawdown and simultaneous exposure. The budget is a *maximum* per-trade stop risk, not a guaranteed risk or a permission to exceed a prop firm's actual limits. No $200 V4 optimum is imported.

Only after validation, using data through 2022, construct prop portfolios from validation survivors. The scientific cohort remains intact. For each of four budgets construct three deterministic templates: (1) all survivors with simultaneous entries processed by canonical ID; (2) all survivors but suppress same-direction entries within 5 minutes after the first fill; (3) one representative from each behavior group selected by highest **validation** stress net per active day, tie by ID, where groups are built by discovery+validation daily P&L correlation ≥0.6 using a fixed greedy ID order. Template 3 is a prop subset, not a scientific filter. Evaluate all 12 budget/template combinations on the same two illustrative generic profiles (EOD and intraday), with max simultaneous planned stop risk `2 × budget`, no new entry after a modeled daily loss of $600, and no contract above the profile cap. Suppressed signals and conflicts are recorded. No template count is selected from audits.

A portfolio is `PROP_ACCEPTED_PREAUDIT` only if, on pooled 2010–2022 selection data, (i) ≥70% of valid constituent signals execute after sizing and conflict rules; (ii) ≥200 executed trades; (iii) stress-cost total net >0; (iv) ≥9 positive calendar years among the 13 touched years; (v) each 120-session rolling evaluation profile has estimated pass fraction ≥0.35 and fail fraction ≤0.40, with a month-block bootstrap lower 95% bound for pass-minus-fail >0; and (vi) 95th percentile of 120-session maximum drawdown is <75% of that profile's loss allowance. Monthly-block uncertainty is necessary because rolling starts overlap heavily. If no portfolio meets every criterion, freeze an empty prop set and report no accepted prop configuration. **Every** portfolio meeting them advances; no arbitrary top-5. These are internal generic research thresholds, not any firm's suitability test. A real-firm claim additionally requires verified commission, contract limits and published firm rules before selection is frozen; absent those, status remains illustrative.

Freeze accepted portfolio IDs, member lists, ordering/conflict rules, four-budget sizing, cost/profile versions and all 2010–2022 selection diagnostics before 2023 data opens. In each audit, report every frozen portfolio under both profiles and the same criteria as a prospective performance assessment, but never reselect. Distinguish an individual predictive failure from sizing/coverage and account-path failure.

## 8. Statistical safeguards and deviations

Use one-sided positive-edge tests only where direction was fixed in advance, with long and short as separate hypotheses. The common daily calendar, including zero-trade days, is the inference unit. Do not count overlapping rolling prop starts as independent observations. Causal model labels must mature before their training cutoff; warm-up may use earlier permitted data but no trade from a later partition can train an earlier-stage model. Inspect matching controls for upward drift, time of day, volatility and direction. Calibrate false positives on zero-edge worlds, not on V4 survivor counts. A path-preserving null may retain real continuation, so failure against it is a limitation, not automatic disproof.

Log implementation fixes, exclusions and exploratory analyses in `ledgers/RESEARCH_DECISION_LOG.jsonl` with timestamps. After a stage opens, no changed rule can be promoted into its frozen cohort. Any post-hoc analysis is labelled `POST_HOC` and cannot alter frozen decisions. A study where size control or causality fails is suspended until the defect is fixed on unexamined data or synthetic worlds. A study where only power fails proceeds with honest underpowered labels and no weakened gate.
