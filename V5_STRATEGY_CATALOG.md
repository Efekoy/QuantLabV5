# V5 mechanism catalog — design for review

At the precalibration science freeze, `quantlab5/v5/signals.py` and
`quantlab5/v5/candidate_inventory.py` are the executable definitions of every
family and A/B/C specification. The prose below explains intended mechanisms;
it does not authorize a different signal, parameter, entry or exit when wording
is less precise than the pinned code. The code-generated candidate IDs are in
`reports/V5_CANDIDATE_INVENTORY.json`.

This catalog defines 30 **Stage A prototypes**, not a run log. Every feature uses only completed 1-minute NQ/ES bars at decision time `t`; an order may fill no earlier than the next bar open. All rolling estimates exclude the current bar unless explicitly called an event completed at `t`. `r_t=log(C_t/C_{t-1})`; `s` is the sign of the NQ 15-minute return; `v` is the median absolute 1-minute NQ return over the preceding 20 sessions at the same minute of session (positive floor: one tick / current price). `z(x)` means `(x - trailing 60-session same-clock median)/(1.4826 × trailing MAD)`, with an epsilon floor and no current-session observations. `q_p(x)` is the causal p-quantile from the previous 60 sessions at the same clock. All session boundaries use New York time. Disallow trading on insufficient warm-up, missing synchronization, or roll-volume masks. Use a common baseline: RTH entries 09:35–15:15; next-bar market entry, stop 1 × prior completed 15-minute ATR, exit at 60 minutes or 15:55, whichever first; no target, no same-bar reentry. Report long and short separately and combined only when the rule defines both. Stage A's simple prototypes make mechanisms comparable; Stage B/C can expand only by the frozen grammar specified before results.

`C15`: first 15-minute NQ move in a direction `s` with `|sum r| ≥ 1.5 × v × sqrt(15)`, with the event's final minute `t`; never a free-standing V5 hypothesis. A state rule below either gates `C15` or generates a distinct event. State thresholds are fixed prototype values, not tuned from later outcomes. For ratios, zero denominators disable the signal. Percentile and robust-z estimates are causal.

| ID | Mechanism, Stage A event/state at `t` | Distinct claim and main failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Event-time continuation: divide preceding same-day NQ volume into causal buckets each containing the prior 20-session median volume for 5 RTH minutes; after 3 completed buckets, require their signed net returns all align with `s`, then take `C15` in `s`. | Persistent movement per traded volume; bar volume is only a proxy for transactions. |
| E02 | Directional-change overshoot: confirm a `1.5v sqrt(15)` reversal from the running 60-minute extreme; after confirmation, take the first same-direction overshoot of half that size. | Tests post-turn continuation, not simple N-bar return. Extremes may be noisy. |
| E03 | Volatility forecast surprise: EWMA(20) forecast of next 15-minute absolute return made at `t-15`; require realized absolute return/forecast ≥1.5 and `C15`. | Forecast error, not generic high volatility; may just price costs. |
| E04 | Online change point: two-sided CUSUM on standardized 1-minute returns with reference 0.5, threshold 4, reset at session open; first upward/downward alarm and `C15` same direction. | Detects fresh state changes; repeated alarms risk dependence. |
| E05 | Regime age: classify sign of EWMA(30) return each minute; require a new sign state between 5 and 20 minutes old and `C15` aligned. | State age rather than state alone; age can be an arbitrary discretization. |
| E06 | First-passage speed: after session open, time to first ±`2v sqrt(30)` displacement; require passage in ≤15 minutes and `C15` aligned. | Fast barrier crossing may indicate urgent flow; barrier is time dependent. |
| E07 | Occupation time: in last 30 minutes, fraction of closes above their 30-minute starting price ≥0.8 for long (≤0.2 for short), plus `C15`. | Tests persistence of path occupancy, not net return alone. |
| E08 | Pullback duration/recovery: after `C15`, wait for counter-move ≥0.5 of impulse over ≥3 minutes, then recovery to prior impulse endpoint within 10 minutes; enter on recovery. | Tests replenishment after a timed pullback; path-state look-ahead must be avoided. |
| E09 | Volume-time toxicity proxy: within the last 30 completed minutes, sum `sign(r)×volume` / sum volume has magnitude ≥0.6 and agrees with `C15`. | OHLCV directional imbalance proxy, **not** true order flow; roll artifacts possible. |
| E10 | Price-impact state: last 15-minute `|sum r|/sum volume` exceeds its causal same-clock 80th percentile and aligns with `C15`. | High movement per traded contract; denominator changes at rolls. |
| E11 | Session handoff: direction of Asia (18:00–02:00), Europe (02:00–08:00), and US premarket (08:00–09:30) net returns agree; first RTH `C15` aligned. | Sequential handoff, not overnight range/gap; clocks and DST matter. |
| E12 | NQ/ES tail dependence: both standardized 15-minute returns exceed their own prior-60-session 90th percentile in the same sign, with NQ `C15`. | Joint extreme alignment rather than correlation; rare events may lack power. |
| E13 | NQ/ES relative value: rolling 20-session regression of synchronized 1-minute NQ returns on ES, fitted through prior session; residual 15-minute cumulative return exceeds 2 prior-session residual SDs, enter in residual direction. | Spread dislocation; hedge ratio instability. |
| E14 | Relative regime transition: NQ and ES EWMA(30) signs disagreed for ≥10 minutes and become aligned at `t`; require NQ `C15` aligned. | State transition rather than generic SMT/lead-lag. |
| E15 | Trend age/exhaustion: count minutes since last CUSUM alarm E04; `C15` only when age 5–20, while age ≥60 is a preregistered negative-control stratum. | Beginning versus exhaustion; alarm definition may dominate. |
| E16 | Volatility term structure: ratio of EWMA 10-minute realized variance to EWMA 120-minute realized variance crosses upward through 1.5 at `t`; require `C15`. | Change in volatility scale, not generic ATR level. |
| E17 | Multiscale volatility: realized variance of 1-minute returns divided by realized variance of nonoverlapping 5-minute returns over last 60 minutes ≥1.5; require `C15`. | Micro-path fragmentation; OHLC sampling effects. |
| E18 | Liquidity-seasonality residual: NQ volume below its prior-60-session same-minute 20th percentile and range above its 80th percentile on two consecutive completed minutes; `C15` direction. | Thin-liquidity movement; bars cannot reveal depth. |
| E19 | Market-state transition probability: fit a 3-state sign/volatility Markov table on previous 60 sessions; take `C15` when estimated probability of a same-sign next state ≥0.65, with Laplace smoothing. | State persistence probability; unstable rare transition counts. |
| E20 | Hazard continuation: among prior-60-session same-clock impulses like `C15`, estimate smoothed probability of reaching +0.5R before −0.5R within 30 minutes; require ≥0.60. | Time-to-event information; outcome windows must be purged and strictly historical. |
| E21 | Targeted TAKE/SKIP: fit regularized logistic continuation outcome model to *only* prior-session C15 events, using E03/E07/E10 states; walk forward monthly with a 1-session purge; take when predicted probability ≥0.60. | Narrow meta-model rather than generic ML; calibration and training leakage risk. |
| E22 | Independent-state agreement: require `C15` plus E07 occupancy and E03 surprise, where both states were computed from distinct features; all three directions agree. | Complementary evidence; correlated feature agreement can be illusory. |
| E23 | Causal Kalman trend: constant-velocity state-space filter with process/observation noise fixed from prior 60 sessions; take `C15` only if filtered trend sign agrees and magnitude exceeds 2 posterior SDs. | Filtered latent trend rather than moving average; hyperparameter fitting risk. |
| E24 | Distribution shift: Jensen-Shannon divergence between histogram of last 30 signed standardized returns and previous 20-session same-clock reference exceeds 0.15; require `C15`. | Distributional change; bins are fixed before outcomes. |
| E25 | Extreme-move clustering: at least 3 of last 20 absolute standardized 1-minute returns exceed previous-60-session 95th percentile; signed majority agrees with `C15`. | Clustering of tail events; can be generic volatility. |
| E26 | Intraday excursion geometry: from RTH open, favorable excursion / (favorable + adverse excursion) ≥0.8 at `t`, with at least 30 minutes elapsed and `C15` aligned. | One-sided excursion path, not plain open distance. |
| E27 | Range-expansion topology: 15-minute range ≥1.5 × preceding 60-minute median 15-minute range, and ≥70% of that range was formed in the final 5 minutes; `C15` aligned. | Late range formation; overlapping windows cause dependence. |
| E28 | Return-volume elasticity: slope of log(abs return + tick floor) on log(volume +1) over previous 60 completed minutes exceeds its previous-60-session same-clock 80th percentile; require `C15`. | Changing impact elasticity; noisy minute returns. |
| E29 | NQ/ES cross-market elasticity: slope of standardized NQ returns on synchronized ES returns over last 60 minutes exceeds its prior-session 80th percentile; require `C15` with ES same-sign move. | Elasticity state rather than lead-lag; correlated-market common trend. |
| E30 | Continuation-failure predictor: at the **completed E08 recovery event**, a monthly logistic model uses the now-observed pullback duration, E16 term structure and E18 liquidity residual to predict stop-before-+0.5R from the recovery entry; take only if predicted failure probability ≤0.35. | Predicts *when continuation fails*; labels must mature before training cutoff. The recovery event, not the earlier C15 instant, is the decision time. |

These are hypotheses, not independent families for multiplicity purposes: overlap is measured and all 30 Stage A tests enter the joint null. E12, E20, E21, E30 may be too sparse or too expensive; eligibility and power calibration decide whether they can support a claim, with results still reported. E09/E10/E18/E28 use **bar volume proxies**, never claim actual order-book liquidity or toxicity. Before discovery, the implementation must pin every estimator (initial state, fit window, histogram bins, tie handling), exact candidate IDs, and enumerated Stage B/C expansions in machine-readable config. Until that exists, this catalog is a reviewable design, not authorization to search.
